## 2.0.0 — 1 August 2026

### Official policy catalogue

- Added source, effective-period, verification, and `official | assumed` metadata per dataset.
- Added complete sourced contribution schedules for 2023–2027, default SPR Year 1/2 G/G schedules, allocation rates, OW/AW rules, published BHS through 2026, and retirement sums through 2027.
- Public reference endpoints now return `404` for unsupported policy years instead of silently clamping or extrapolating.
- Policy responses now use 24-hour edge caching with stale revalidation instead of year-long immutable caching.

### Contribution calculation

- Added `contributionMonth`, `ordinaryWages`, optional `additionalWages` with required annual ceiling context, `citizenship`, and either `age` or `birthMonth`.
- Implemented the official S$50/S$500/S$750 wage bands, month-after-birthday transition, integer-cent calculation, contribution rounding, employee-share truncation, and employer-as-remainder rules.
- Responses now identify the wage band, effective schedule, post-55 RA routing branches, warnings, and provenance.
- Age bands use inclusive `maxAgeInclusive` bounds. RA is returned explicitly after the 2025 SA closure.

### Projection and CPF LIFE

- Replaced annual shortcuts with a monthly ledger and required `startMonth` plus starting OA, SA, MA, and RA balances for new clients.
- Added per-year policy status and explicit fixed-salary, floor-interest, retirement-routing, and frozen-future-policy assumptions.
- Replaced OA-to-SA-only transfers with age-aware `retirementTransfer`.
- Removed SimplyCPF's personalised CPF LIFE estimate. `cpfLifeReference` now contains CPF Board's exact 2026 male Standard Plan rows; deprecated `cpfLifeEstimate` is always `null`.

### Interest rates

- Removed the unsourced monthly SGS series.
- `/interest-rates` and `/interest-rates/trend` now expose official quarterly CPF declarations from 2023 Q1 through 2026 Q3 with first-party source URLs.
- Renamed the SMRA input to `averageSgsYield` to make the official 12-month-average methodology explicit.

### Compatibility window

Accepted for one release cycle, with migration warnings:

- `income` → `ordinaryWages`
- `date` → `contributionMonth`
- `oaToSaTransfer` → `retirementTransfer`
- `sgsYield` → `averageSgsYield`

Additional Wages without annual OW/prior-AW context now return `422`.

## 1.x

The original API exposed contribution, projection, age, ceiling, interest, and investment endpoints. It used annual projection shortcuts, ambiguous age bounds, a synthetic monthly SGS series, and a SimplyCPF CPF LIFE estimate. Those behaviours are superseded by v2.
