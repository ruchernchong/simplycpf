# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Singapore employees and Permanent Residents who want to understand CPF contributions and plan CPF outcomes without needing financial jargon, an account, or prior policy knowledge.

## Product Purpose

SimplyCPF turns dated, first-party CPF policy into transparent calculators and planning tools. Success means users can see where their money goes, understand the assumptions behind projections, and distinguish official references from SimplyCPF estimates.

## Brand Personality

Careful, plain-spoken, and trustworthy. The product should feel calm and exact without impersonating the CPF Board or presenting estimates as financial advice.

## Anti-references

Avoid sales-led fintech dashboards, opaque recommendation engines, decorative data visualisations, generic card-heavy SaaS layouts, and any treatment that suggests SimplyCPF is an official government service.

## Design Principles

- Make the numbers and their provenance the main event.
- Explain policy in plain English without hiding important caveats.
- Keep related inputs, results, and assumptions visibly grouped.
- Prefer focused tools and progressive disclosure over dense dashboards.
- Earn trust through specificity, consistency, and explicit scope.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, clear labels, keyboard-operable interactions, readable contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and Singapore English. Do not make a formal conformance claim without a dedicated accessibility audit.
